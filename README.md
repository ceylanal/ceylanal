# Ali Salih Ceylan

I study Management Engineering at Istanbul Technical University. I build software for financial research, public-disclosure monitoring, and business operations.

## Selected projects

- **[Company Intelligence GraphRAG](https://github.com/ceylanal/company-intelligence-graphrag)** — Searches 30 annual reports from 10 Borsa Istanbul companies using vector search and a knowledge graph. Answers cite source PDF pages. [Retrieval example](https://github.com/ceylanal/company-intelligence-graphrag/blob/main/docs/assets/retrieval_demo.png)
- **[KAP Bildirim](https://github.com/ceylanal/kap-bildirim)** — Monitors Türkiye’s public market disclosures and sends Telegram and email alerts. A persistent SQLite queue supports delivery retries after restarts. [Architecture and quick start](https://github.com/ceylanal/kap-bildirim#architecture)
- **[Ocula / OkurLab](https://github.com/ceylanal/ocula)** — A Turkish reading app for PDF and EPUB files with annotations, reading drills, and in-browser narration. Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase. [Live demo](https://ocula-read.vercel.app)
- **[NutriTrack](https://github.com/ceylanal/NutriTrack)** — A prototype for dietitians and clinics to explore meal plans, client records, and appointment workflows. React, TypeScript, Supabase. [Live demo](https://nutri-track-nu-hazel.vercel.app)
